Built for Indian payments from day one. Lowest gateway fee at 1.8% via Razorpay partnership. No 3rd-party plugin headaches like Shopify merchants face.
Loyalty points native
Earn + redeem loyalty at checkout. Shopify merchants pay ₹1,200–3,000/mo for this. Zoho needs Zoho Thrive add-on. ZKCommerce includes it free.
Shipping labels native
Print-ready Delhivery labels, bulk printing, barcode, QR code — no paid app. Shopify merchants pay ₹800+/mo. Zoho needs Shiprocket integration.
Full analytics on all plans
Revenue, traffic, UTM, conversion funnel, product performance, top cities — on every ZKCommerce plan. Shopify restricts analytics to ₹5,599/mo Grow+ plan.
Unlimited staff & warehouses
Shopify limits to 2–15 staff, 4–8 locations. Zoho limits to 2–5 users, 1–3 locations. ZKCommerce gives unlimited staff and warehouses on all plans.
India support — phone + WhatsApp
Shopify India offers chat/email only. Zoho offers email/chat. ZKCommerce provides local phone & WhatsApp support by ZKanadavel Infotech's India team.
