Setup steps

1. Create Meta Business account at business.facebook.com
2. Create a Facebook App at developers.facebook.com
3. Create a Product Catalogue in Commerce Manager
4. Add META_APP_ID, META_APP_SECRET, META_CATALOGUE_ID to Railway Variables
5. Get a long-lived access token from Meta Business Manager
6. Add META_ACCESS_TOKEN to Railway Variables
7. Click "Sync products now" above
