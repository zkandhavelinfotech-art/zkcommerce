Zero LABSA. Zero compromise.
LABSA (Linear Alkylbenzene Sulphonic Acid) is linked to skin irritation and potential carcinogenic effects. We are one of the first Indian dishwash brands to completely eliminate it — replacing it with plant-derived surfactants that clean just as powerfully.

Our formula uses plant-based surfactants and detergent enzymes that are 100% biodegradable. They break down naturally after use — safe for drains, waterways, and the environment. Cleaning your dishes should not cost the planet anything extra.
