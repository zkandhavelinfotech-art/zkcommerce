  • 🎨 Themes & Visual Customizer (merchant/themes.html): Active theme spotlight, color swatches, 10-theme preset library, and direct 1-click launch into the StoreBuilder editor.
  • 🚀 Standalone Multi-Cloud Bundle: Bundles the Merchant Admin, StoreBuilder, and auth into self-hosted exported packages (GoDaddy cPanel, Railway, Vercel) with standalone Express API.
