How to Use
Best results in 4 steps
🌡️
1
Warm the oil
Gently warm a small quantity in your palms or in hot water for 2 minutes. Warm oil penetrates deeper and faster.
💆
2
Massage scalp
Apply from roots outward. Massage for 5–10 minutes with fingertips — this stimulates blood circulation and herb absorption.
🌙
3
Leave overnight
For best results, leave on overnight. Wrap hair in a warm towel for 20 minutes to deep-condition before sleeping.
🚿
4
Wash thoroughly
Wash with a mild, sulphate-free shampoo next morning. Use 2–3 times a week for visible results in 4–6 weeks.
