Brand concept
The ZKCommerce mark
The logo fuses the ZK lettermark (representing ZKanadavel Infotech) with a commerce symbol — a bold geometric arrow rising through a hexagon, representing trade, upward growth, and the precision of modern ecommerce. The hexagon references Tamil Nadu's artisan heritage and the structured grid of a marketplace. The rising arrow inside it represents merchant growth and transactions flowing upward.

Brand guidelines
Do's and don'ts
Keep the ZKCommerce mark strong and consistent.

✓ Do

Use "ZKCommerce" as one word, capital Z, K, C
Use Fire Orange (#FF6B00) on dark or white backgrounds only
Keep the hexagon mark and ZK lettermark together
Use Syne 800 for all display type
Maintain clear space of at least 1× the hex height around the logo
Use the white version on dark/colour backgrounds
Always pair "ZK" bold with "Commerce" regular-weight

✗ Don't

Don't use "ZK Commerce" (two words) or "Zk commerce"
Don't place the logo on coloured backgrounds other than brand orange or black
Don't stretch, rotate, or warp the hexagon mark
Don't use other fonts for the wordmark
Don't use the logo smaller than 24px height
Don't use orange text on an orange background
Don't separate "ZK" from "Commerce" in the wordmark
